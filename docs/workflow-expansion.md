# Workflow expansion — September 26, 2026

This release keeps **one workspace per account**. Implementation and verification use local synthetic data only. No hosted migration, deployment, Git push, or AI provider request was performed.

## Delivered behavior

- **Payment corrections:** an owner/admin or owning freelancer can correct an erroneously recorded receipt with a reason. The original payment remains, an immutable correction is added, and the invoice balance/status and PDF recompute from unreversed receipts. This is a bookkeeping correction, not a refund or money transfer. Identical request retries are safe; a second different correction is rejected.
- **Time billing:** completed work from one project/client can become an invoice draft in one transaction. Business work requires independent approval. Each entry is reserved once, with an immutable snapshot, and cannot be changed while reserved. Charges use exact minutes rounded to cents per entry. Delete an unissued draft or cancel an issued invoice to release time; cancelled history stays available. Legacy `invoice_id` links remain reserved. Time-generated lines are fixed; recreate a draft to change them.
- **Review inbox:** review captured expenses/time, independently approve or reject submissions, and follow up on personal or vendor bills. Rejected records can be corrected and resubmitted. The database now verifies the actual approval transition; the former policy could silently affect zero rows.
- **Money-out history:** status changes and allowlisted financial values are recorded without receipt paths, notes, or contact details. Paid/cancelled bills and approved expenses cannot be rewritten or deleted by browser clients. Expenses/vendors are archived and bills cancelled to preserve records. Vendor bill headers and lines save atomically and totals are calculated in the database. Payments require a recorded date and do not initiate transfers.
- **Duplicate protection:** personal bills reject an exact matching payee/name/date/amount/currency; vendor bills serialize duplicate vendor/bill numbers; receipts cannot be attached to a second active expense on creation. The expense UI retains its request ID and uploaded receipt across retries. This is not general fuzzy duplicate detection or document-content hashing. Ambiguous non-invoice saves instruct the user to reload and inspect the result; they do not all replay a successful result like invoice commands do.
- **Workspace registry:** stable organization IDs identify business workspaces and user IDs identify individual workspaces. A unique membership per user is backfilled and protected from client forgery. Protected legacy profile changes synchronize membership role/active state. Switching established workspaces requires a separate migration operation. Existing financial ownership columns/policies remain as a compatibility interface; this is not a wholesale ownership-column cutover.
- **Contacts:** one searchable, paginated, RLS-preserving directory over clients and vendors, with archived visibility and links to their editors. Existing IDs and roles remain distinct; possible duplicate people/companies are not automatically merged. Vendor location/payment-term fields now persist instead of being discarded. Vendor tax IDs are excluded from the common directory.
- **Settings and reporting:** validated preferences persist using atomic patches. New manual invoices use saved tax/currency/payment terms, including zero-day terms. Tax Prep separates calendar year, currency, and cash receipts versus issued invoices; reversed payments and draft/cancelled invoices are excluded. Cash reports do not invent payments for legacy PAID invoices. Expenses are captured records, not proof of payment or tax deductibility. Concurrent quarterly-reminder creation does not duplicate periods or replace existing amounts. Unsupported sales-tax placeholder totals are hidden.
- **Security:** per-request CSP nonces protect scripts, connections are limited to the configured Supabase origin, and page responses are private/no-store. Caller-supplied nonce headers are overwritten. Inline styles remain permitted for Radix positioning/charts/theme behavior; development alone permits script evaluation. Pages now render dynamically, with the associated server cost/cache tradeoff. Deferred accounting/payroll/inventory modules remain disabled; database grants also deny their browser writes, while historical reads/exports require the manager and matching parent ownership. Team permission management remains deferred.

## Verification

- TypeScript (`npm run lint`; this is not ESLint), 27 unit/document/reporting/security tests, and optimized production build.
- Disposable PostgreSQL fresh install and upgrade/restore suites: original columns across twelve historical fixture tables are preserved; backup restores into a separate database. Role/tenant/inactive-account denial, forged membership, forbidden workspace switching, concurrent settings/reminder saves, actual approval status, immutable history, duplicate/version checks, atomic bill saves, payment corrections, and concurrent time reservations are exercised.
- Deferred-module tests cover all nine fixture actors across owner/admin/member/contractor/freelancer/personal/inactive/foreign users. Browser writes are denied; malformed cross-workspace journal/payroll parent relationships are hidden.
- Local real Auth/API checks execute actual application services for all three account types, membership/settings, independent review, bills/vendor bills, archived contacts, retry protection, PDF downloads, receipt storage/expiry, and 1,005-record pagination. Page responses receive distinct CSP nonces and reject forged nonce headers.
- In-app browser: approve a synthetic expense and inspect its history; save and reload 7.5% tax and zero-day terms, then verify invoice prefill; distinguish $300 cash received from $310 issued; filter out other years/currencies. Contacts and Tax Prep fit a 390px viewport. Payment correction and 61-minute/$100-per-hour time billing produced an exact $101.67 draft. Browser warnings/errors were checked separately from automated tests.

Repeatable commands are in [the foundation release notes](workflow-foundation.md). `npm run test:reporting` adds report calculation and CSP unit coverage. `npm run test:release:local` requires the local stack and locally configured running app; it refuses non-loopback services. Synthetic API fixtures are retained locally for inspection.

## Remaining boundaries and release gates

1. **Production readiness is not established by local tests.** Hosted upgrade/restore rehearsal, representative historical-data inspection, production migration-history reconciliation/recovery point, older-client inventory (including iOS), preview-domain checks, hosted email/expiry flows, and production security settings remain pending under the local-only instruction. The app and all eight timestamped workflow migrations must release together; old direct-write clients are incompatible.
2. Legacy payment reconciliation still requires actual evidence and user review. No receipts, historical approvals, missing vendor fields, or invalid historical records were fabricated or rewritten. Existing older receipt URLs may remain valid until their original expiry.
3. The tax estimator is explicitly illustrative, single-filer, USD-only, and limited to reviewed 2025/2026 rules. It excludes credits, QBI, capital gains, other wages, additional Medicare tax, state tax, retirement deductions, disaster relief and safe-harbor calculations. Fiscal-year/date-format preferences are stored but not applied globally; reports disclose calendar-year scope. Notifications are stored preferences, not an email-delivery implementation.
4. Advanced accounting posting, payroll calculation/payment, inventory changes and team administration require dedicated audited commands before re-enabling. Purchase-order commands and remaining legacy modules are not certified by the new vendor-bill workflow. This release does not certify Amountly as a complete accounting/payroll/tax system.
5. Live AI success/failure and browser-level response-loss fault injection remain unverified. API retry/concurrency tests pass; the AI provider is deliberately disabled locally.

## Pre-commit follow-up

Additional checks pass for competing approve/reject and pay/cancel actions (one transition/event), simultaneous duplicate vendor bill creation (one bill), and deliberately lost successful responses for bill, expense, vendor-bill and payment-correction requests. The actual application services are exercised against local Supabase; reload/retry preserves one record. This is transport-level fault injection, not browser offline emulation. The upgrade/restore fixture now includes existing vendors, paid personal/vendor bills, vendor bill lines and a EUR bill.

A database trigger now rejects new bills for archived vendors, serializes against concurrent archival, and preserves payment of their existing bills.
